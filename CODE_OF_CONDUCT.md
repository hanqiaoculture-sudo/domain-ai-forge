# Code of Conduct

We want Domain AI Forge to be practical, rigorous, and welcoming.

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain domain assumptions clearly.
- Avoid sharing private data, credentials, or sensitive customer content.
- Make room for both AI engineers and domain experts.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or intentionally disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed to protect the community.
